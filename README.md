## Taishoku Reset — Japanese exit-agency SaaS

A production SaaS for 退職代行 (exit-agency services) in Japan, operated through
LINE with secure web forms, AI-assisted voice/translation and an internal
operations workflow.

- **Role:** Co-Founder & Tech Lead
- **Status:** in production since 2026-11-08
- **Scope of this case study:** product context, public flow, high-level
  architecture, engineering challenges and ownership boundaries.
- **Not included:** source code, D1 schema, prompts, pricing logic, operator
  panel, incident documents, customer data, conversion metrics, internal
  endpoints or analytics IDs.

## What these case studies are

- A description of the **problem**, the **public product flow**, the
  **architecture at box level**, and the **engineering trade-offs**.
- A record of what I owned and what I did not.
- A pointer to public evidence: live sites, public repositories, public legal
  pages and public social channels.

## What they are not

- They are not a code dump. Client code and company code stay private.
- They do not contain customer data, credentials, internal metrics, prompts,
  database schemas or operator screens.
- They do not claim ownership of a company's legal representation, commercial
  terms or customer relationships.
